You can type exact values in the fields next to the sliders (confirm with Enter or by leaving the field). Gain 1.0 is about ISO 100, gain 4.0 about ISO 400. A short exposure (for example 1/1000 s) freezes moving cars, and gain makes up for the missing light. The exposure cannot be longer than one sensor frame. The camera rounds the exposure time and the gain to its own steps (for example 1000 µs may become 992 µs). The real values are shown in the "Current" line and in frames.csv.
For a data set, a fixed white balance (daylight, or "Lock current") is better than automatic, because automatic changes the colours between frames.
